# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React, Vite, Tailwind and Framer Motion. Accessible Radix UI components.
## Product Purpose
A public, fictional 24-hour canal-city bakery concept called Nachtbakkerij, showcasing oven schedule, menu, four night scenes, and visit information.
## Users
Assumption from the brief: late-night walkers and early risers looking for something warm and a welcoming place.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Static GitHub Pages site at /night-bakery/. Fictional names, menu, prices and address are explicitly labeled. Schedule is a local-clock demonstration, not inventory. No ordering, tracking, paid services or sign-in. Responsive at 390px and desktop; reduced-motion support.
## Brand Commitments
Premium night bakery, oven-glow hero and canal-city setting.
## Evidence on Hand
The assigned guide describes project setup, Framer Motion, written design taste and component libraries. No real business or assets supplied.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, semantic landmarks, clear focus and contrast, prefers-reduced-motion.
## Assumptions
Subagent implementation from the explicit brief without a human interview. All business material is invented demonstration content.
